At 01:24:00 EST on March 26, 2024, the Singapore-flagged container vessel M/V Dali experienced a complete loss of electrical power and propulsion while departing the Port of Baltimore along the Patapsco River shipping lane [§Claim-104]. The harbor pilots on board transmitted a "Mayday" distress signal via VHF Channel 13 to the Maryland Transportation Authority Police, enabling officers to prevent further motor vehicular traffic from traversing the Francis Scott Key Bridge roadway deck within 90 seconds of the notification.

At 01:28:45 EST, the vessel made direct physical contact with Pier 18, a critical reinforced concrete support pylon. The continuous truss superstructure suffered immediate structural discontinuity, precipitating the progressive collapse of three spans into the 50-foot navigation channel [§Claim-109]. At the moment of impact, an 8-person road repair crew contracted by Brawner Builders was actively filling potholes on the center span. Emergency personnel rescued two workers during initial surface operations; six personnel remained unrecovered during morning search diving protocols.

Subsequent air quality, volatile chemical, and water sampling carried out by the Maryland Department of the Environment identified 56 containers holding regulated hazardous materials aboard the vessel, with no breaches of containment or hazardous leaks detected in river transects [§Claim-112]. Commercial maritime traffic into and out of the Port of Baltimore was suspended indefinitely by the Captain of the Port via Coast Guard Broadcast Notice to Mariners.
